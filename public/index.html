FIELD TEST 002 / OVERLANDING

Make a way through.

Click the house, a tree, a rock, timber, or the ground to open its action wheel. Choose Drive, Step outside, Attach cable, Fell tree, Dig, or Place soil. Keyboard shortcuts are optional.

A / D
Walk, or throttle at the wheel. SHIFT holds the brake. Leaving the wheel applies the parking brake.
E
Board, use a station, or leave it. Walk to the far left of the cabin to exit.
Space / S
Space jumps on foot. At the winch, Space reels in and S pays cable out. A solo driver can operate the winch too.
Click ground
Choose Dig or Place soil, then hold the mouse to shape the ground. Right-click or Esc returns to the action wheel. Keys 1 / 2 also work.
Click anchor
Walk to a tree or rock and choose Attach cable. Choose Release cable to detach. Disabled actions tell you when to move closer.
Click tree
Choose Fell tree to keep sawing until it falls. Move or press Esc to stop. Its trunk becomes physical timber.
Click timber
Choose Pick up. Click again to rotate or set down. Q / R and E still work. Logs can support wheels, slide, or roll.
Mouse wheel
Zoom out to scout. H reopens this guide.

Try this first: park before the pine, get out, and fell it. Move the log out of your way or use it on the approach. Climb ahead to the ridge tree to attach the winch, then trade jobs. You can also reshape the climb with your shovel.

If the house tips over, use the visible Right the house button or its action wheel. It restores everyone upright on nearby clear ground; earthworks stay. Recover is also available from anywhere. This is a simplified 2D physics test with fixed cabin construction, not an engineering simulator. Trips reset on server restart.
A HELPING HAND

Back to solid ground?

Recovery works from anywhere. It returns the house and both travelers to safe ground near the last turnout, releases the cable, and drops carried logs. Your terrain work stays in place.
